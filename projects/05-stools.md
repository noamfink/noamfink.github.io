## Concept

The concept of a stool arrived not alone, but as a pair, a reflection: two forms in quiet dialogue. An invitation for connection, conversation, and unity.

![The two stools, light oak and dark walnut, in a sunlit white room](images/mdf-lamination-stools/01.png)

## Process

Crafted through curved MDF lamination using heat, and finished with oak and walnut veneer, the form reflects the fluidity and warmth of companionship: two pieces that belong together, yet retain their own identities.

![MDF layers clamped over a curved form](images/mdf-lamination-stools/02.jpg "Clamping the lamination")

![MDF sheet inside a heating press](images/mdf-lamination-stools/03.jpg "Heat forming")
