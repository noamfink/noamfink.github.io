## Overview

This project explores the intersection of algorithmic generation and intentional human touch: a visual and structural dialogue between the computer's raw logic and the designer's aesthetic will. Built using parametric modeling and generative design principles in Autodesk Fusion, the chair acts as a physical manifestation of computational forces shaped by human curation.

## Concept & design process

The core narrative of the chair centers on relinquishing structural control to the machine, only to reclaim it through artistic expression.

- **Algorithmic foundation:** Simulated weight loads and structural constraints were applied directly to the seat, legs, and backrest zones. The software was tasked with computing an organic, optimized load-bearing network capable of safely supporting a human body's weight.
- **Structural evolution:** The algorithm organically carved away excess material and grew bridging pathways, resulting in an unconventional, bone-like topology that prioritizes structural efficiency over rigid symmetry.

![The chair in three-quarter view on a grey studio backdrop](images/generative-design-chair/02-three-quarter.jpg)

## The clash of computer and human design

While the computer determined where the material was strictly necessary for survival under load, the project truly comes alive in the tension between digital efficiency and human intent. Once the algorithmic skeleton was generated, the design was actively curated and refined to reflect a distinct aesthetic perspective.

The resulting form is a deliberate clash: the fluid, unpredictable, and mathematically optimized morphology dictated by computational stress-testing collides with deliberate, sculpted human intervention. The piece questions where machine-driven optimization ends and artistic authorship begins, resulting in a functional sculpture born from both code and hand.
