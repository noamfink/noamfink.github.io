## Concept

In our fast-paced routines, even the smallest daily rituals often go unnoticed. This juicer was designed to interrupt that rhythm, encouraging the user to slow down and rediscover the quiet beauty in everyday actions.

Its spiral form turns juicing into a visually stimulating experience: as you press the orange, droplets trace a winding path downward, drawing attention to the flow and motion of the juice. The design invites pause and presence, transforming a familiar task into something poetic and meditative.

![Video of the juicer in use](images/orange-juicer/video.mp4 "The juicer in use")

## Result

Designed to be used from both sides and to sit securely on any glass, it allows juice to flow directly into the vessel, no need for transfer. Crafted to balance elegance with utility, this piece reimagines a kitchen tool as a tactile and aesthetic ritual, a gentle reminder to find beauty in the process and intention in the mundane.
