## Problem

Asthma affects approximately 1 in 10 children worldwide, making it one of the most common chronic conditions in early childhood. This interactive nebulizer reimagines treatment as a calming, responsive experience designed specifically for young users.

![Side view of the nebulizer mask and compressor beside the project introduction](images/interactive-childrens-nebulizer/01.png)

## Concept

To optimize medicine delivery, the nebulizer synchronizes mist production with the child's breathing patterns, releasing medication only during inhalation. This reduces both energy use and medication waste while improving overall treatment efficiency. A soft LED glow mirrors the rhythm of each breath, offering visual feedback that helps guide and soothe the child through each session.

![The pebble-shaped mask connected by a cable to the white compressor unit, with mist](images/interactive-childrens-nebulizer/02.png "Pulse: mask and compressor unit")

![Line drawings of a child and parent using the nebulizer at home](images/interactive-childrens-nebulizer/03.png "Use scenario")

## Result

The nebulizer is connected to a compressor unit topped with a soft, pink LED feature that inflates and deflates in sync with the child's breathing. This pulsating light provides visual feedback, helping the child stay engaged and more at ease throughout the process. When the mask is removed, the light gradually fades, signaling inactivity and reinforcing the connection between the child and the device.

Minimal in form and warm in tone, the nebulizer is designed to blend into the home while offering a sense of comfort and familiarity, turning clinical care into a soothing, interactive experience.
