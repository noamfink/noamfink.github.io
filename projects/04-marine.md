## Problem

Traditional marine tagging methods are often invasive and harmful, posing risks to animal welfare and altering natural behaviors.

## Concept

This project proposes a non-invasive tagging solution inspired by the suction mechanism of the remora fish. An autonomous marine drone approaches the animal and deploys a soft suction-based tag, minimizing stress and behavioral disruption.

## Result

The goal is to shift marine research toward more respectful, biomimetic methods that prioritize animal welfare and scientific accuracy. By reducing physical interference, this approach supports more reliable insights into marine life and ecosystems.
