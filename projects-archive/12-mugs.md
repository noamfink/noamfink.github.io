## Concept

This series of mugs rethinks the way we hold and are held by everyday objects. Starting from the handle, I explored new ways to create a more intimate, comforting interaction between the mug and the user.

Each mug is designed with distinct finger placements: some allow the hand to slip through openings, others invite a full grip or a gentle cradle. These gestures go beyond function; they create a physical connection between user and object.

## Result

Just as the user wraps their hand around the mug, the mug responds with soft curves and contours that press back into the hand, offering comfort, warmth, and a subtle sense of being embraced in return.

Through form and touch, the mugs aim to turn a daily routine into a moment of quiet connection.
