## Concept

This concept reimagines The North Face retail space not just as a store, but as an immersive terrain, a physical and emotional landscape that invites exploration. Every element, from the flooring to the product displays, echoes the textures and challenges of the natural world, encouraging customers to test, touch, and fully engage with the environment.

Using Grasshopper as a parametric design tool, we developed a generative code that produces a textured pattern formed by overlapping circles. Where these circles intersect, a unique, topographic-like surface emerges, reminiscent of natural formations such as rock ridges, compressed snowfields, or dense forest canopies. This algorithmically generated pattern was refined, scaled, and integrated into the spatial design and material finishes, grounding the concept in both data-driven precision and organic inspiration.

## Variable Terrain Testing Zone

The Variable Terrain Testing Zone is an engineered retail flooring system designed to simulate real-world hiking conditions within the store environment. Composed of modular surface segments that replicate diverse trail textures, such as gravel, rock, mud, incline, and uneven terrain, it allows customers to assess footwear performance under conditions that mimic outdoor movement. Integrated with subtle topographic gradients and non-slip materials, the platform enables precise evaluation of grip, stability, and stride adaptability.
